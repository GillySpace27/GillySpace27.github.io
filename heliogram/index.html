Heliogram

The live Sun on your Mac's screens: as your desktop picture, your screen saver, or a wall of video.

Download for MacApple Silicon and Intel

macOS 13 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel

Until Heliogram 0.8 is out, this downloads Heliograph 0.7, the same app under its old name.

  • Open the disk image and drag Heliogram to Applications.
  • Already have it? Drag the new one over the old; your settings stay.
  • From 0.7 on, Heliogram keeps itself up to date. Earlier versions show a notice in the menu bar Sun; download this one over them.

iPhone, iPad and Android

The App Store version is the one with Kiosk, for lobbies and classrooms. iPhone and iPad come to the same App Store page next; Apple TV is planned.

Download for AndroidAndroid 8 or later · phones and tablets, with a widget
  • Tap the button on your phone. When Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then open the downloaded file and tap Install.

What's new in 0.7

  • Heliogram now updates itself. It checks once a day, downloads in the background and installs when you quit; a kiosk installs overnight.
  • Lighter on your network: imagery is checked once a day, or twice if you choose, only what your screens show is downloaded, and nothing is fetched unless it has changed.
  • What you pick is what you see. Un-ticking a product takes it off every screen; ticking one fetches it at once.
  • On a wall, the arrow keys step through the clips, and no wavelength appears on two screens at once or twice in a row.
  • A redesigned Settings window that follows dark mode, shows the version, and has a Turn Off and Quit button that undoes what Heliogram set up.
  • A new icon.
