Heliogram

The live Sun on your Mac's screens: as your desktop picture, your screen saver, or a wall of video.

macOS 13 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel

Install with Homebrew

Homebrew currently has Heliogram .

Install

brew tap GillySpace27/tap
brew install --cask gillyspace27/tap/heliogram

Update

brew update
brew upgrade --cask gillyspace27/tap/heliogram

If Homebrew refuses the tap as untrusted, run brew trust gillyspace27/tap once, then install again. Needs macOS 13 or later. Heliogram also keeps itself up to date.

  • Open the disk image and drag Heliogram to Applications.
  • Already have it? Drag the new one over the old; your settings stay.
  • Heliogram keeps itself up to date.
  • Coming from Heliograph 0.7? It will not update itself to Heliogram: install this one, then move Heliograph to the Trash.
