What's new in 0.7

  • Heliogram now updates itself. It checks once a day, downloads in the background and installs when you quit; a kiosk installs overnight.
  • Lighter on your network: imagery is checked once a day, or twice if you choose, only what your screens show is downloaded, and nothing is fetched unless it has changed.
  • What you pick is what you see. Un-ticking a product takes it off every screen; ticking one fetches it at once.
  • On a wall, the arrow keys step through the clips, and no wavelength appears on two screens at once or twice in a row.
  • A redesigned Settings window that follows dark mode, shows the version, and has a Turn Off and Quit button that undoes what Heliogram set up.
  • A new icon.

For lobbies and classrooms

Kiosk mode turns any Mac behind a TV into a screen that shows the Sun as it was in the last 48 hours, all day, with no one tending it.

  • Starts at login and stays up; only Control-Option-Command-Q closes it.
  • Runs offline: everything is downloaded first, so the screen is never blank.
  • Hides the Dock and menu bar, and captions each clip with its source.
  • A QR code on screen lets visitors take home a print of the exact image they are looking at.
