Heliogram terms of use

Draft, not yet in effect

Personal use is free

Desktop, Screen Saver and Wall are free for personal use on any Mac you use yourself, at home or at work.

Showing Heliogram in public needs Kiosk

Showing Heliogram on a screen meant for the public or for visitors (a lobby, a classroom, a museum, a shop window, a waiting room or an event) needs an active Kiosk subscription for each Mac driving such a screen. The subscription is the licence for that public display. One subscription covers one Mac and every screen connected to it.

The two Kiosk subscriptions

Kiosk lets you choose whether the myheliograph.com link and its QR code appear on screen. Kiosk with Store costs less and keeps that link and QR code on screen while Kiosk runs; turning them off needs Kiosk.

Billing

Kiosk is sold by Apple through the Mac App Store as an auto-renewing monthly subscription. Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase, and the subscription renews each month unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You manage and cancel it in your Apple ID's subscription settings. Refunds are handled by Apple under its own policies. Apple's standard licence agreement also applies to the App Store version.

A Mac that cannot reach the App Store keeps running Kiosk for 7 days after a subscription ends, so a renewal it has not yet seen does not blank the screen. When the subscription has lapsed, Kiosk closes the next time Heliogram starts.

The imagery

The pictures of the Sun come from NASA's Solar Dynamics Observatory and are processed by gilly.space for display. They are a visualization, not calibrated scientific measurements. The licence covers the Heliogram app and its presentation; it does not grant or restrict any rights in NASA's data.

No warranty

Heliogram is provided as is. New imagery depends on NASA, Amazon Web Services and Cloudflare, and may be late or missing at times.
