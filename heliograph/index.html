Heliograph

The live Sun on your Mac's screens: as your desktop picture, your screen saver, or a wall of video.

Download for Mac

macOS 13 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel

  • Open the disk image and drag Heliograph to Applications.
  • Already have it? Drag the new one over the old; your settings stay.
  • From 0.7 on, Heliograph keeps itself up to date. Earlier versions show a notice in the menu bar Sun; download this one over them.

What's new in 0.7

  • Heliograph now updates itself. It checks once a day, downloads in the background and installs when you quit; a kiosk installs overnight.
  • Lighter on your network: imagery is checked once a day, or twice if you choose, only what your screens show is downloaded, and nothing is fetched unless it has changed.
  • What you pick is what you see. Un-ticking a product takes it off every screen; ticking one fetches it at once.
  • On a wall, the arrow keys step through the clips, and no wavelength appears on two screens at once or twice in a row.
  • A redesigned Settings window that follows dark mode, shows the version, and has a Turn Off and Quit button that undoes what Heliograph set up.
  • A new icon.
